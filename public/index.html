Analyst verification

Mark each keyword Good or Not good and give the reason it suits, or does not suit, our page. Tick Must use on the secondary keywords the page has to contain (at least one); the RD, Publish and Live checks then require them. Manager approval

Approved keywords move to the Research List with the approval date and the must-use keywords. Approval uses the account you are signed in with, plus the manager PIN.

Research List

Approved keywords, New report or Revamp, must-use keywords, live status from the sitemap, and the latest live check.

RD verification

Pick the approved report (its primary, secondary and must-use keywords fill in) and paste the RD. Every use of the keywords is highlighted in colour with a count, a missing must-use keyword is reported as an error, and the content is checked for data consistency, grammar and facts. Each check is saved so the whole team can read it in Check results.
