How the SEO Workbench works

A report idea moves through six steps until its page is live. Each step reads from a few outside services and logs what it found to one Google Sheet. Pick a step to see what goes in and what comes out.

The tour moves to the next step every few seconds. Click any step to stop on it.

          Checking a fact, step by step

          Facts are the most expensive thing to check, so the tool spends as little as it can on each one. Most facts end up costing nothing.

            Two kinds of checks

            Some problems can be found with exact rules. The rest need an AI reviewer, whose findings are tested before you see them.

            Exact rules

            The same answer every time, at no cost.

            • The CAGR matches its own start and end values
            • A share matches its value and the market total for that year
            • Every mention of the market size matches the Overview
            • "Majority" is only used for more than 50%
            • No missing space after a full stop, no double spaces, no repeated words
            • Every paragraph ends with a full stop
            • No "MEA" or "the US", and no region or abbreviation written two ways

            AI review

            Reads the text the way an editor would.

            • Spelling and grammar
            • The same figure given two different values
            • Missing sections, and regions the text never discusses
            • FAQ answers that differ from the description
            • Infographic numbers against the text
            • Report Snapshot and Table of Contents against the text

            Before an AI finding is shown

            • The text it quotes must be on the page
            • Its fix must change something
            • Notes that say there is no conflict, and requests to double-check, are dropped
            • A region or segment figure is never compared with the market total
            • The reviewer gives its most likely answer every time, so a rerun matches

            If a finding in the RD tab is not an error, untick its Error box. The decision is saved with your email, and the whole team sees it the next time the report is checked.

            Who can do what

            Everyone signs in with their Kings Research email. A few actions are kept to named people, set in the tool's settings.

            Everyone

            Uses every tab after signing in. The first sign-in uses the starting password, then each person sets their own.

            In-house keyword editors

            Add hand-picked keywords to the pipeline after seeing their volume, scope and a reason.

            Managers

            Approve or reject keyword groups, using the manager PIN. Approved reports go to the Research List.

            Accounts owner

            The only person, besides the tool itself, who can open or change the sign-in accounts.
